Frequently Asked Questions

What are your gym hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and weekends from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes, we provide one-on-one personal training sessions. Contact us to schedule yours.

What should I bring to my first workout?

Bring comfortable workout clothes, water bottle, and a towel. We provide lockers for your belongings.

Are there any age restrictions?

Members must be 16 years or older. Minors under 18 require parental consent.
